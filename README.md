I'm **Ioannis (Giannis) Fountoulakis**, a seismologist and postdoctoral researcher at the [Institute of Geodynamics, National Observatory of Athens](https://www.gein.noa.gr/en/).

### 🔬 Research
 
- **Earthquake source imaging**: tracking how a rupture grows in space and time, using back-projection of high-frequency seismic waves (my PhD topic)
- **Aftershock sequences**: high-resolution catalogs and monitoring of sequences such as Samos 2020 and Santorini–Amorgos 2024–25
- **Ground motion and site effects**: how local geology shapes what a station records, and why that matters when imaging ruptures
- **Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS)**: fibre-optic cables used as dense arrays of seismic sensors
- **Computational seismology and AI**: GPUs, HPC and machine learning for faster, automated seismology
- **Seismic anisotropy**: shear-wave splitting in the crust and mantle, where my research started

**[SSA2py](https://github.com/ifountoul/SSA2py)** (lead developer)<br/>
The Source-Scanning Algorithm in Python, for rapid imaging of earthquake sources in near-real time. It pulls waveforms from FDSN web services and runs on GPUs or multi-core CPUs.<br/>
